## 💼 Project Overview
**CrossBorder Solo** is a zero-friction international invoicing and GST LUT export compliance engine engineered specifically for solo freelancers, software consultants, designers, and remote professionals billing clients in the US, UK, Europe, Australia, and UAE.

### 🌟 Key Differentiating Capabilities:
1. **Automated Indian GST LUT Statutory Declaration:** Automatically injects the mandatory 0% export GST declaration required by Indian tax authorities (*"SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT OF SERVICES UNDER LETTER OF UNDERTAKING (LUT) WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX"*).
2. **Dual-Currency Real-Time Estimation:** Displays billing currency in USD/EUR/GBP/AUD/CAD to foreign clients while providing instant Indian Rupee (INR) reference equivalents for domestic bookkeeping.
3. **Print-Perfect A4 Vector PDF:** Uses standard `@media print` engine to output razor-sharp, corporate Fortune-500 grade PDF invoices with zero third-party branding.
4. **Polite Payment Follow-Up Nudge:** 1-click generator for polite, high-converting WhatsApp and Email reminders for clients with upcoming or overdue invoices.
5. **No Subscription Lock-In:** A clean, 100% private, client-side tool sold at a **one-time ₹199 / $5.00** lifetime license instead of $30/month SaaS subscriptions.
